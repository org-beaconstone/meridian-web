## Certification checklist

The item below is the Whiteboard addition. One item covers the whole module. Do not split import, conversion, and summarise into separate certifications.

- [ ] **Whiteboard.** In the first 30 days, the rep delivers the [Whiteboard module](new-rep-onboarding-playbook-v2026.04/whiteboard.md) from the [New Rep Onboarding Playbook v2026.04](new-rep-onboarding-playbook-v2026.04/index.md). A manager watches the 20-minute demo, or reviews the unassisted practice, and confirms all three beats on a synthetic board: import from Miro as editable stickies, sticky-to-Jira conversion, and Rovo summarise (**Summarize what I'm looking at**, then **Create page** or **Copy summary**). Evidence is a link to the whiteboard, a link to one created work item, and a link to the summary. A Loom recording is not required.

Sign-off is the manager's name and the date, written next to the item. Skip sign-off when the summary beat did not happen, including when Rovo is off for the site. Import and conversion alone do not clear the item.

Curriculum placement: [days 1–30](30-60-90-curriculum.md).

# Provider expansion: source context

[Existing Confluence playbook](https://beacon-stone.atlassian.net/wiki/spaces/SPT/pages/147947579) is authoritative. [Google working draft](https://docs.google.com/document/d/1KeMKQyaUu-sLtFqwvm3cI8zoZo_QwXe7yDvB9qK3n54/edit) is supplied but its contents were not readable through current access.

The Confluence rubric assigns regulatory standing25, market coverage20, technical fit20, commercials15, reliability10 and support10. Below70 is rejected;70-79 needs a written exception;80+ proceeds to sandbox. Mandatory policy no-go criteria cannot be traded away for a commercial score.

Phases: sandbox contract/failure/webhook/reconciliation evidence; tightly capped pilot with risk review and clean reconciliation; staged full ramp with support readiness. No next provider is selected or preimplemented. The original repo's suggested75-point threshold is superseded by this canonical80-point rule.

See [compliance policy](compliance-policy.md), [API standards](api-standards.md) and [connected workspace](workspace-map.md). All controls are fictional demo context, not evidence of real bank/provider compliance.
